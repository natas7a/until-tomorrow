Who we are

We are a small team of young thinkers, builders, and advocates, driven by the belief that we should have a role in shaping the future.

Until Tomorrow operates at the intersection of technology, ethics, and leadership. As artificial intelligence and emerging tech rapidly change the global landscape, traditional education is struggling to keep up. We are stepping in to ensure youth have a seat at the table, equipped with the critical thinking and adaptive skills required to navigate and govern tomorrow's world.

Child Safety & AI Fellowship

Young people are entering a world being reshaped by artificial intelligence, demographic change, climate pressures, and automation. Yet they are often positioned as recipients of these changes rather than as people who can actively shape what comes next.

The Child Safety & AI Fellowship introduces participants to anticipatory leadership and futures thinking as practical tools for engaging with complex and uncertain futures, through their application to the specific topic of AI and Child Safety.

Eligibility Ages 18–30, from anywhere in the world.
Cohorts Three small cohorts for deep, discussion-based learning: Cohort 1, Cohort 2 and the Peace & Policy cohort.
Schedule Six weekly 1.5-hour sessions on Saturdays, from 10 October to 14 November 2026. Each cohort meets at its own time, chosen for its members’ time zones.
Format Highly interactive rather than lecture-based, with activities such as story circles, and readings or videos before each session.
The work Identify trends, map systems, build future scenarios, and design concrete actions. The programme culminates in a collaborative model-UN-style simulation during the final session.
Partners Organised in partnership with the Asia-Europe Foundation (ASEF), drawing on methodology from the Copenhagen Institute for Futures Studies and the Development Academy of the Philippines.

Cohorts & facilitators

Natasha Litherland designed the fellowship, including its six-step foresight method, session plan and materials, and leads it. All three cohorts follow that method and plan. Aliki Ioannides designed and leads the Peace & Policy cohort, which applies the method to children in war, conflict and crisis, with its own reading list and exercises.

Six weeks, six steps

Every cohort meets once a week on these Saturdays, for exactly 1.5 hours.
WeekDateSessionRead first
Week 1Sat 10 OctIntroduction: meet your cohort, the topic and the methodNo reading: bring one question with you
Week 2Sat 17 OctFraming & Scanning: choose a focal issue and collect signals of changeWeek 2 prep
Week 3Sat 24 OctSensemaking: map how the signals connectWeek 3 prep
Week 4Sat 31 OctScenarios: build four possible futures for 2040Week 4 prep
Week 5Sat 7 NovBackcasting & Refinement: plan back from a desired future, then stress-test itWeek 5 prep
Week 6Sat 14 NovSimulation game: a model-UN-style negotiation using everything so far, led by Iqra Mumtaz for all cohortsWeek 6 prep
